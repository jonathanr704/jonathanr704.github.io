Project Overview

About the Project

SweetTech Gear is an independent gaming and technology platform focused on helping users explore gaming equipment, product information, buying guides and relevant offers.

The project combines a responsive front-end interface with a PHP back-end and a relational database. This structure supports dynamically generated product pages, categorized content and product offer management.

An administrative interface is being developed to manage products and support future expansion of the website's content and features.

The platform is being designed with mobile usability in mind, allowing visitors to browse technology content across phones, tablets and desktop computers.
